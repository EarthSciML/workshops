About the Workshop

Join us at IIT Bombay for a two-day, hands-on workshop on the future of atmospheric and Earth system modeling. Learn to build, compose, and extend scientific models using EarthSciML and ModelingToolkit.jl — open-source Julia libraries that let researchers create modular, equation-based model components and seamlessly combine them into complex simulations.

Day 1 grounds participants in atmospheric science and data science fundamentals, while Day 2 offers a deep dive into the EarthSciML ecosystem with tutorials, collaborative brainstorming, and a hackathon where you'll build your own model components.

No prior Julia or EarthSciML experience required — all exercises use interactive computational notebooks and instructors will guide you every step of the way.

This workshop is designed for graduate students, postdocs, and researchers interested in atmospheric modeling, environmental engineering, and computational Earth science. Participants at all programming skill levels are welcome. Travel support may be available for selected participants.
